Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

Synapse is a personal, non-commercial study app run by one person, not a company. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no data is ever sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

You can use Synapse without signing in. Signed out, everything below about accounts and servers simply does not apply — your progress is stored only in your own browser and never leaves your device.

What signing in shares

Sign-in is handled entirely by Google. If you choose to sign in, Google provides this app with four things:

  • A permanent identifier for your Google account
  • Your email address
  • Your name
  • A link to your Google profile picture

The app never sees your Google password, and never requests access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar, or any other Google service.

What is recorded while you study

For each flashcard or question: how many times you have seen it, how many times you answered correctly and incorrectly, which review stage it currently sits in, and the date it is next due. Alongside that: your completed session history (date, mode, and score), which days you studied, your current streak, and your app settings.

Where it is stored

On a server hosted by Railway in the United States, in a file identified by your Google account identifier. A cookie named hub_session keeps you signed in for up to 180 days; it contains a random session reference and nothing else. It is not used for tracking, and there are no advertising cookies on this site.

The activity log

When you finish a quiz or test while signed in, one row is added to a private Google spreadsheet belonging to the person who runs this app. That row contains the time, the subject, your name, your email address, the name of the session, the mode, and your score.

The spreadsheet is private and is not published anywhere. If you would rather not appear in it, use the app signed out — it works fully without an account.

What is never collected

  • Your password
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, files, or messages
  • Anything you do on other websites
  • Advertising or cross-site tracking data of any kind

Deleting your data

Reset Progress, at the bottom of any subject page, erases your stored progress — both the copy in your browser and the copy on the server.

To have everything removed, including your account record and any rows already written to the activity log, email the address below and it will be deleted.

Who this is for

Synapse was built for one student and their classmates. It collects only what is listed on this page, only from people who choose to sign in, and only for the purpose of tracking their own study progress.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated.
